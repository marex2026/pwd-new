Wine Collection Moving

Moving a wine collection without risking it

Packing, inventory support, refrigerated transportation, short-term in-transit holding when needed, unloading, and cellar placement for private collectors, estates, and relocation partners.

What a move involves

A collection is not ordinary freight

Wine is heat-sensitive, fragile, heavy, and often irreplaceable. A standard moving truck can sit in the sun for hours; a general carrier may cross-dock a shipment several times. Neither is acceptable for a cellar you spent years building.

We plan the move around the wine: inventory before pickup, wine-specific packing, careful hand loading, temperature-controlled transportation, and as few transfers as possible.

We also work directly with moving and relocation companies, taking only the wine portion of a household or corporate move so the rest of the relocation can proceed normally.

Included in a collection move

  • Inventory assistance. Documentation of what is being moved before it leaves.
  • Wine-specific packing. Proper materials and labeling; we can pack, or move wine already in original wooden cases (OWC) or shippers.
  • Hand loading and unloading. Careful handling at both ends, including stairs and cellar access.
  • Temperature-controlled transport. Refrigerated equipment and temperature requirements planned for the route.
  • Short-term in-transit holding coordination. For closing-date gaps and cellar build-outs when needed.
  • Cellar placement. Unloading and placement at destination, not just a stack of boxes in the garage.

Skipping zone

A Texas skipping zone for the Southeast

Our DFW hub can act as a skipping zone for shipments continuing beyond Texas. This can reduce origin-side packing requirements and shorten the parcel portion of the route.

Wine can arrive refrigerated at our DFW hub, where PWD can repack it, add ice packs when appropriate, and release it to an authorized parcel carrier. This reduces the parcel portion of the route and limits exposure to summer heat.

When combined with refrigerated line haul, the skipping-zone model keeps wine temperature controlled for a larger portion of the transportation route.

Rail & intermodal

A rail-connected option for larger wine shipments

For qualifying larger-volume shipments, PWD can coordinate rail and intermodal transportation through vetted logistics partners, including coordinated access to a rail-served DFW facility for qualifying Union Pacific-connected freight. Railroad, routing, equipment, schedule, and temperature requirements are confirmed for each shipment.

Depending on the lane and equipment, the movement can combine rail-served receiving, intermodal drayage, transloading, cross-docking, pallet handling, and refrigerated truck transportation for the next or final leg.

This option is designed for wineries, importers, distributors, fulfillment operations, and other high-volume shippers evaluating alternatives to long-haul truck-only transportation.

How the pieces fit together

  • Rail-served receiving for qualifying inbound freight.
  • Drayage between rail/intermodal terminals, warehouse facilities, and onward carriers when required.
  • Transloading between rail, truck, and warehouse operations.
  • Cross-docking & consolidation through the DFW logistics network.
  • Refrigerated local/final-mile transportation for qualifying Texas movements.

Availability depends on route, shipment volume, rail equipment, transload requirements, schedule, and required temperature conditions.

About PWD

Wine people, running wine logistics

Premium Wine Delivery® is a TABC-permitted wine carrier specializing in temperature-controlled wine transportation, collection moving, and wine logistics services. Serving the wine industry since 2016.

PWD started with a simple idea: wine deserves a delivery experience designed around the product. Conventional parcel and general-freight networks are not built around temperature-sensitive bottles, narrow receiving windows, or the expectations of premium wine customers.

Our team gained firsthand experience with conventional wine-delivery networks and saw the same recurring challenges: limited delivery visibility, broad time windows, repeated handling, summer heat exposure, and added fees.

PWD was built as the alternative. Today we combine refrigerated Texas delivery, private collection moving, DFW cross-dock and consolidation, commercial wine logistics, and coordinated interstate solutions.

Privacy Policy

How Premium Wine Delivery handles information submitted through this website.

Effective date: August 12, 2026.

Premium Wine Delivery® is operated by PWD Services LLC, a TABC-permitted carrier in Farmers Branch, Texas. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, contact us, or track a shipment, we may collect contact details, pickup and delivery addresses, shipment information, declared value, service preferences, and anything else you choose to tell us.

When we perform a delivery we record Proof of Delivery (POD): the recipient's signature, the name of the person who accepted the shipment, the time, one or more photographs of the delivered wine at the delivery point, and location data where our system captures it. Deliveries of alcohol require an adult signature, so our driver checks government-issued photo identification at the door. We record that ID was verified. We do not retain a copy of the document.

Information About Other People

Most shipments involve someone other than the person booking them. If you give us a recipient's name, address, or phone number, you confirm you are entitled to share it with us. We use it only to complete and document that shipment, and we handle it the same way we handle yours.

How We Use Information

To prepare quotes, plan and perform transportation, communicate about shipments, provide tracking and proof of delivery, take payment, keep business records, and meet legal, tax, insurance, and alcohol-carrier obligations.

Email and Text Messages

When you give us a phone number for a shipment, we may text you about that shipment — scheduling, driver ETA, delivery confirmation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop shipment texts, or HELP for assistance. We will not send you marketing or promotional texts unless you separately agree to receive them.

Tracking Look-ups

Shipment status, signatures, and delivery photographs are only shown after both a tracking number and a matching email address or phone number are entered. This is deliberate: a tracking number alone will not reveal delivery details.

Service Providers and Logistics Partners

We share information with service providers and logistics partners only as reasonably necessary to quote, arrange, transport, track, insure, or deliver a shipment. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

We may also disclose information where required by law, by TABC or another regulator, or to establish or defend a legal claim. PWD does not sell personal information, and does not share it with advertisers.

Website Data

Our host records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages viewed, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party ad tracking. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.

Retention and Security

We keep business and shipment records as long as reasonably necessary for operations, customer service, claims, accounting, insurance, and legal obligations, and then delete or archive them. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that a transmission over the internet is.

Your Choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it, stop shipment texts by replying STOP, or unsubscribe from any email we send. Ask through the contact details in the footer of this site and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Adults Only
